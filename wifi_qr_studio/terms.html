Simple and transparent terms for using our Wi-Fi card generator and scanner.

Usage and Functionality

WiFi QR Studio is provided as a utility to help users format, design, print, and scan standard Wi-Fi network QR codes. All generation and rendering occur locally on your device.

Offline Operation & Security

WiFi QR Studio operates 100% offline without requiring accounts, sign-ins, or cloud subscriptions. You are solely responsible for ensuring that any Wi-Fi passwords and credentials you print or display are intended for guest access in accordance with your network policies.

Disclaimer of Warranty

WiFi QR Studio is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. While engineered for maximum compatibility with Android, iOS, and standard barcode readers, device camera quality and local lighting conditions may influence barcode scanning performance.
