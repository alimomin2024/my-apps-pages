Terms of use

Simple terms for using our automotive telemetry utility.

Usage and Safety

Speedo HUD is provided to display vehicle speed, acceleration benchmarks, and trip metrics. Always drive safely, obey local traffic laws and speed limits, and never interact with your device while driving. Mount your device securely before operating your vehicle.

Offline Operation

Speedo HUD operates 100% offline without requiring accounts, logins, or subscriptions. All configurations and trip odometer statistics are stored strictly on your device.

Disclaimer

Speedo HUD relies on your device's internal GPS receiver and satellite reception. Tunnel transit, dense foliage, tall buildings, or hardware limitations may momentarily impact GPS speed accuracy.
