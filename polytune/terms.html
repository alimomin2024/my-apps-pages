Using PolyTune

Terms of use

Simple terms for using our precision instrument utility.

Usage and Functionality

PolyTune is provided as a musical instrument tuner and metronome utility. It operates entirely on your device using mathematical pitch detection algorithms.

Offline Operation

PolyTune operates 100% offline without requiring internet access, accounts, or subscriptions. All settings and tuning presets are saved locally on your device.

Disclaimer

PolyTune is provided "as is" to assist musicians in tuning instruments and keeping time. While engineered for sub-cent mathematical precision, microphone hardware quality and background ambient noise may influence pitch detection responsiveness.
